How we make our dum biryani

Dum is the slow way: the biryani is sealed in its pot and left to cook in its own steam.

The method

Four steps, no shortcuts

  1. 01

    Marinate

    The chicken rests overnight in yoghurt, ground spices and fried onions.

  2. 02

    Par-cook the rice

    Aged 1121 basmati is boiled with whole spices until it is about seventy percent done.

  3. 03

    Layer and seal

    Rice goes over the meat with saffron milk and ghee. The handi is closed with a ring of dough.

  4. 04

    Dum

    Forty-five minutes on low heat. The rice finishes in the trapped steam.

Ingredients

What goes in

  • Aged 1121 basmati
  • Desi ghee
  • Kashmiri saffron
  • Chicken, marinated overnight
