Dum biryani

Chicken and aged basmati, layered in a clay handi and slow-cooked under a dough seal.

01The method

Sealed and slow-cooked

Each handi is sealed with dough and cooked on low heat for 45 minutes. The steam stays inside, so the rice takes on the flavour of the chicken and spices.

02Ingredients

What goes in

  • Aged 1121 basmati
  • Desi ghee
  • Kashmiri saffron
  • Chicken, marinated overnight

03The menu

From the handi

Chicken, mutton, veg and egg dum biryani, with mirchi ka salan and double ka meetha on the side.
