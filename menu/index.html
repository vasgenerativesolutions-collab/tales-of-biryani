Menu

Every biryani is layered and sealed in its own handi, then cooked on dum.

  • Chicken dum biryani

    Bone-in chicken, marinated overnight and layered with aged basmati.

  • Mutton dum biryani

    Goat on the bone, slow-cooked in the sealed handi until tender.

  • Veg dum biryani

    Seasonal vegetables and paneer, layered with saffron rice.

  • Egg biryani

    Boiled eggs in a spiced masala, layered with basmati and fried onions.

  • Mirchi ka salan

    Green chillies in a peanut, sesame and tamarind gravy. Served on the side.

  • Double ka meetha

    Fried bread soaked in sweetened milk, finished with ghee and dry fruits.
